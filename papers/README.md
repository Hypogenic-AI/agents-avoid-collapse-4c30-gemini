# Downloaded Papers

1. [AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data](2305_17493_model_collapse.pdf)
   - Authors: Ilia Shumailov, Zakhar Shumaylov, Yiren Zhao, Nicolas Papernot, Ross Anderson, Yarin Gal
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2305.17493
   - Why relevant: Foundational paper on model collapse showing that training generative models recursively on their own generated data causes original distribution tails to disappear.

2. [Cooperate or Collapse: Emergence of Sustainable Cooperation in a Society of LLM Agents](2402_04259_cooperate_collapse.pdf)
   - Authors: Giorgio Piatti, Zhijing Jin, Max Kleiman-Weiner, Bernhard Schölkopf, Mrinmaya Sachan, Rada Mihalcea
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.04259
   - Why relevant: Addresses the hypothesis from the perspective of an LLM agent ecosystem managing a common resource. Investigates whether multi-agent systems can achieve sustainable cooperation without collapsing.

3. [Epistemic diversity across language models mitigates knowledge collapse](2407_08480_epistemic_diversity.pdf)
   - Authors: Damian Hodel, Jevin D. West
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2407.08480
   - Why relevant: Directly addresses the research hypothesis by investigating if increasing the diversity of models in an AI ecosystem can mitigate collapse.

4. [Data Augmentation using Large Language Models](2402_11894_data_augmentation.pdf)
   - Authors: Bosheng Ding, et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.11894
   - Why relevant: Reviews how LLM-generated data is used for further training and data augmentation, highlighting the challenges of maintaining data diversity and avoiding collapse.

5. [Model Collapse Does Not Mean What You Think](2408_06456_model_collapse_revisited.pdf)
   - Authors: Rylan Schaeffer, Joshua Kazdan, A. Arulandu, Oluwasanmi Koyejo
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2408.06456
   - Why relevant: Provides a critical reassessment of the model collapse literature, arguing that different interpretations of model collapse affect future generative models in ways that may be more avoidable in real-world scenarios.
